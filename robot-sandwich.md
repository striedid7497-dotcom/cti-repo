# Robot Dream Italian Sandwich Algorithm

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing upright in front of a clean, empty kitchen counter.
- A whole, pre-sliced horizontal loaf of fresh Italian bakery bread rests on the counter with the top and bottom halves separated and open crumb-side up.
- Squeeze bottles or jars with spoons of Chick-fil-A sauce, mayonnaise, and vinaigrette are on the counter with lids off.
- Slices of pepper jack cheese, provolone cheese, black forest ham, and pepperoni are placed in separate containers on the counter.
- Prepared bowls of shredded lettuce, sliced tomatoes, sliced black olives, sliced pickles, and sliced pickled jalapenos are on the counter.
- A decorative toothpick skewered with a pimento-stuffed green olive is on the counter.
- A clean plate is placed flat in front of the robot.
- The robot understands physical grasp, scoop, place, layer, squeeze, and release commands.

## Numbered Steps
1. Pick up the bottom half of the Italian bread loaf and place it crust-side down on the center of the plate.
2. Using fingers, gently grasp and pull out the soft interior bread crumb from the center of the bottom half to create a hollow trench, placing discarded crumb aside.
3. Pick up the Chick-fil-A sauce bottle and squeeze an even layer into the hollowed trench of the bread.
4. Pick up the mayonnaise bottle and spread a layer directly over the Chick-fil-A sauce.
5. Pick up the vinaigrette bottle and drizzle an even layer over the mayonnaise.
6. Pick up 1 slice of pepper jack cheese and lay it flat across the sauce.
7. Pick up 1 slice of provolone cheese and lay it overlapping next to the pepper jack.
8. Pick up a second slice of pepper jack cheese and lay it flat next in line.
9. Pick up a second slice of provolone cheese and lay it flat to complete the cheese foundation.
10. Pick up 1 slice of black forest ham and lay it flat over the cheese.
11. Pick up 2 slices of pepperoni and lay them over the ham.
12. Pick up another slice of black forest ham and lay it over the pepperoni.
13. Pick up 2 more slices of pepperoni and lay them flat to finish the meat layer.
14. Grasp a handful of shredded lettuce and scatter it evenly across the meat.
15. Pick up 2 tomato slices and place them on top of the lettuce.
16. Grasp a small handful of lettuce and place it on top of the tomato.
17. Pick up 2 more tomato slices and arrange them across the lettuce.
18. Pick up a handful of sliced black olives and scatter them evenly across the top.
19. Pick up 4 pickle slices and arrange them in an even row across the roughage.
20. Pick up 4 jalapeno slices and distribute them evenly across the pickles.
21. Pick up the top half of the Italian bread loaf and hold it crust-side down in your left hand.
22. Drizzle vinaigrette across the open interior surface of the top bun.
23. Squeeze an even zigzag of Chick-fil-A sauce over the vinaigrette.
24. Spread a layer of mayonnaise over the Chick-fil-A sauce on the top bun.
25. Turn the top bun upside-down so the sauced surface faces downward.
26. Carefully lower and align the top bun directly over the loaded bottom half on the plate.
27. Press down lightly with the palm of your hand on the center of the top bun to seat the sandwich.
28. Pick up the toothpick holding the olive with pimento.
29. Push the pointed end of the toothpick vertically through the exact center of the top bun until the olive rests against the crust.
30. Step back and rest arms at your sides.

Done When: A fully assembled, layered Italian sandwich rests on the plate, held together in the center by an olive-pimento toothpick, with all sauces and ingredients fully enclosed inside the bread.
